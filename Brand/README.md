## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create the production iPhone app icon for the existing app Nourish, a personal calorie and meal journal. One single square opaque 1024x1024 image, full bleed solid deep forest green background, no rounded outer corners (iOS applies its mask). Center one memorable, bold, minimal cream-white emblem combining a circular dinner plate with an abstract sprouting leaf, subtle lime accent on the leaf. Geometric, flat vector-like shapes, premium calm editorial wellness identity, crisp edges, strong recognizability at 40px. Symbol occupies about 62% of canvas with generous clear margins. No letters, words, gradients, shadows, mockup, border or extra symbols. Palette matches a forest green and warm ivory interface. No transparency.
